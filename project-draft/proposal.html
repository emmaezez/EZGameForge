PlayForge: A Centralized Platform for Game Discovery & Publish

A web app where developers can publish their games and players can explore, comment, and save what they love.

Problem / Data Domain

Independent game developers often struggle to find accessible, low-barrier platforms to share their work and connect with audiences. Existing sites like Steam, itch.io provide vast marketplaces, but they often prioritize visibility based on sales or paid promotion rather than creativity or community engagement.

As a result, many independent developers struggle to gain visibility for their creative projects, especially in early development or testing stages. While these platforms focus on profit and market trends, players who want to discover innovative, experimental, or in-progress games often find it difficult to do so. This creates a gap between indie developers who want feedback and exposure, and players who are eager to explore fresh experiences.

PlayForge aims to bridge this gap by creating an interactive, user-driven space where developers can publish their own games, and players can browse, review, and build personal collections. Rather than being a static catalog, the site encourages active participation from both sides — developers and players — to foster a sense of shared creativity and collaboration.

APP Description

This project proposes the creation of an interactive front-end web application called PlayForge.

Developers can publish games in progress—including descriptions, demos, and development updates—while players can test, review, and track their favorite titles. This creates a feedback loop that benefits both sides: developers receive real-time insight from their audience, and players gain early access to creative and diverse games that might never appear on mainstream stores.

PlayForge uses React, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the front end, supported by Firebase for real-time data storage and updates.

The app's core features include:

  • Developer Game Upload & Progress Tracking: Developers can post games currently in Alpha, Beta, or Pre-release stages, each with detailed metadata such as genre, development stage, and demo links. They can update progress logs to share development milestones, bug fixes, or new builds over time. In addition, developers can view and organize player feedback directly on their dashboard—allowing them to filter, sort, or prioritize comments and ratings. This helps them track how user opinions evolve throughout the game’s development process and make data-driven improvements.
  • Comment, Review & Moderation System: Players can leave reviews, give ratings, and share improvement suggestions for in-development games. Developers can reply directly to comments or mark certain feedback as “implemented,” creating transparent and ongoing collaboration. Other users can vote comments up or down to surface the most helpful feedback, or reply to specific reviews to start discussions. To ensure a constructive space, the system also allows developers and users to hide or report inappropriate content. All comments and ratings update dynamically, with average ratings and vote counts recalculated in real time, giving both developers and players insight into evolving community feedback and engagement.
  • Personal Wishlist & Tracking: Players can build a personalized library where they keep track of games they’ve saved for later, followed for updates, commented on, or played. Each action contributes to their profile, allowing them to revisit titles they’ve engaged with and see how those games have evolved over time. Game pages display how many users have saved or followed them, helping highlight the most anticipated or active titles in the community. Users can filter by “Most Followed” or “Most Played” to explore trending games.
  • Search & Filtering System: Users can filter games by genre, development stage, or rating to find titles that match their interests—making discovery faster and more personalized. They can also sort results by most followed, most played, or highest rated to explore what’s trending in the community.

The primary users of this application are independent game developers and players who want an interactive and creative space to share and explore games. It is designed for developers who wish to publish their projects, showcase their work, and connect with players directly, as well as for gamers who enjoy discovering community-made titles and supporting indie creators.

Ultimately, this application bridges the gap between developers and players by transforming the game-sharing process into an interactive experience. It encourages creativity and collaboration, allowing developers to gain visibility and players to engage directly with the creators behind their favorite titles. Rather than functioning as a static catalog, PlayForge acts as a dynamic web app that empowers users to publish, explore, and connect in a more personal and meaningful way.
